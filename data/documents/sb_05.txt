Shareback memo: Summit Pack P5 (SPP5-25)

We ran a wear test starting 6 May 2025 with fifteen participants. Comfort was scored 5/5 in the first week, but the overall group rating settled at 4,5 out of 5. Reviewed by Dev and Amara.

Our recommendation is to ship.

Key takeaways:
1. Load transfer stayed comfortable at 12 kg.
2. Hip belt pockets were praised.
3. Zipper pull snagged on the rain cover.

Additional notes: none.
